REFOLLOW

Read your Instagram data on your own phone. No password, no fake login — only the official export file.

Get it on Google Play

ReFollow analyses Instagram’s Download Your Information ZIP on your device (JSON only). It shows who doesn’t follow you back, who follows you, and mutual follows in simple lists. It never accesses the Instagram app.

What you get

  • 📦

    Official ZIP / JSON

    Open the export you download from Instagram. HTML exports are not supported.

  • 📱

    On-device analysis

    Follower lists are not uploaded to our servers. Processing stays on your phone.

  • 👤

    Not following back

    See accounts you follow that do not follow you — in a clear, dedicated list.

  • 💛

    Fans

    See who follows you when you do not follow them, on its own screen.

  • 🤝

    Mutual follows

    Separate two-way relationships at a glance and read a simple health summary.

  • 📈

    Changes and Pro

    New followers, unfollowers, a watchlist, and multi-account tracking in Pro.

Security policies

ReFollow takes a different path so we do not put your account at risk. Password apps can open a fake “Instagram login” — that form is not the real instagram.com.

  • 🚫

    No login

    ReFollow does not sign in to Instagram and never accesses the Instagram app.

  • 🔑

    No password

    There is no username or password form. Analysis uses only the ZIP you choose.

  • 🛡️

    Data stays with you

    Lists are not uploaded. Settings → Delete data clears local records on the device.

  • ⚠️

    Fake-login risk

    Apps that take your password can turn the account into a bot, trigger a ban, or leak messages.

  • 🧒

    Ages 13 and up

    The app is not designed for children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.

  • ⚖️

    Not affiliated with Meta

    ReFollow is not officially affiliated with Instagram or Meta Platforms.

How to use it

  1. 📲

    1. Export

    In Instagram: Download Your Information → format JSON, date range All time.

  2. 📂

    2. Open the ZIP

    When it is ready, load the file in ReFollow.

  3. ✨

    3. Read the lists

    After analysis, who doesn’t follow back, who follows you, mutual follows, and more are ready for you.

The free version may show ads. ReFollow Pro is ad-free; subscriptions are monthly or yearly through Google Play.
