ReFollow

Personal social graph analysis

Get it on Google Play

ReFollow is an app that lets you analyze your Instagram followers and following lists. The app does not log into your Instagram account and does not ask for your password. Analysis is done only using the JSON/ZIP data file you download via Instagram's own "Download Your Information" feature.

  • 🔑

    ReFollow never asks for your Instagram password.

  • 📦

    Analysis uses only the official ZIP (JSON) export.

  • 📱

    Your lists stay on your phone — they are not uploaded.

  • 🚫

    ReFollow never accesses the Instagram app.

With ReFollow you can see:

  • Accounts that don't follow you back (free: first 5; full list Pro)
  • Accounts that follow you but you don't follow (Fans, Pro)
  • Mutual follows (Pro)
  • Follower changes between analyses (Pro)
  • When you followed accounts (from JSON export) and sort by follow date
  • Watchlist — star accounts and get alerts when they change
  • Possible username changes detected between two exports
  • Follow health summary on Home (rates and trends)
  • Encrypted backup & restore — keep snapshots when you change phones (Pro)
  • Shareable summary card (PNG) — ratios and trends without username lists
  • PDF snapshot report — last 5 analyses at a glance (Pro)
  • Multi-account — analyze several Instagram accounts separately (Pro)
  • Export category lists as CSV and share them (Pro)
  • Biometric app lock (fingerprint / Face ID)
  • The free version contains ads. Your first analysis is ad free; later analyses unlock with a short ad.
  • Follow requests you've received; follow requests you've sent that are still pending (Pro)

How to use?

  1. 📲

    Create the export

    In the Instagram app: Profile → top-right menu (☰)
    Accounts Center → Your information and permissions
    Download your information → Create export
    Transfer to device → choose JSON + All time
    Start export

  2. 📂

    Download the ZIP

    Wait 5–15 minutes (Instagram also emails when the ZIP is ready)
    Return to the same screen → tap Download
    Save the ZIP file

  3. ✨

    Run analysis

    On the ReFollow Home screen, upload the ZIP you downloaded from Instagram; analysis starts automatically.

Required: JSON format and All time must be selected.

ReFollow Security Policies

Why we don't risk your account

  • ⚠️

    How password apps work

    These apps open, inside their own screens, a form that looks like an Instagram login page but is fake — it is not the real instagram.com address. The moment you type your password, every keystroke is copied straight to their server. Your password is no longer yours; it is in the hands of the app owner.

  • 🚨

    Risks

    • Your account used as an ad bot (selling likes/follows)
    • Permanent ban by Instagram for “suspicious login”
    • Access to your private messages and photos
  • 🛡️

    The ReFollow difference (ZIP)

    We never ask for your password because we don't go “inside” your account. We process Instagram's official data file (ZIP) on your device. Your data is not uploaded to the internet or a server — it stays only on your phone.

  • ⏱️

    Spending two minutes to upload your data file is far smarter than losing your account.

Privacy

Follower-list analysis stays on your device. Free: ads and a 5-name preview of who doesn't follow back; Pro unlocks the full list, history, exports, and removes ads. Subscriptions go through Google Play. For why we use ZIP instead of passwords, see Security Policies above and the full privacy policy in Settings.

ReFollow is not officially affiliated with Instagram or Meta.
