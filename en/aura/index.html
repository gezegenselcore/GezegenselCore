Highlights

Sample screenshots are below; other key areas are described in text only.

Reflection

Reflection creates short moments for mental pause and self-awareness during the day. It offers simple, actionable prompts aligned with AURA’s current guidance.

Dream Journal

Dream Journal helps you record dreams and notice emotional themes over time. AURA supports interpretation and reflection, but does not provide medical or psychological diagnosis.

Aura Academy

Aura Academy explains energy, focus, stress, and daily awareness through simple educational content. It helps users better understand the guidance AURA provides.

Community

Community is the social layer where AURA users can share experiences, ideas, and their growth journey. It is designed to create support, not pressure.

Privacy First

The baseline stays on your device and account — you see how information is used. Read the full picture in our privacy policy.

Your AI Partner

Aura acts like a smart coach for everyday choices — rhythm, load, and goals in context, with small concrete steps. No spiritual claims or medical promises — just measurable, understandable language.
