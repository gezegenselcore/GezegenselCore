ReFollow — Privacy policy

0. Age limit (13+)

ReFollow is not intended for children under the age of 13 and does not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you believe you are under 13, please do not use the app.

1. Data we process

ReFollow only uses data from the Instagram "Download Your Information" (JSON/ZIP) export file that you choose. That file may contain usernames, follower and following lists, mutuals, and similar information. The app processes this data only on your device; we do not upload your Instagram lists to our servers.

2. Where data is processed

All analysis and processing happens on your device (on-device). Summaries such as followers/following, mutuals, not-following-back, fans, and pending follow requests are produced locally using set operations.

3. Sharing

We do not send your Instagram lists to our servers. Sharing a report or output (for example a screenshot, CSV, PDF, PNG summary, or encrypted backup file) is entirely optional and up to you. We do not sell data to third parties.

4. Retention

Data is stored only on your device. Retention is under your control; you can clear local data using Settings > Delete data. Uninstalling the app also removes local data.

5. Security and account access

ReFollow never accesses the Instagram app. It never asks for your Instagram password and never presents an Instagram login page. Login forms shown by password apps are not the real instagram.com address. Apps that request your password can capture credentials and put your account at risk (for example unauthorized use as a bot, permanent bans for suspicious login, or access to private messages and photos). ReFollow instead analyzes Instagram's official Download Your Information export (ZIP/JSON) only on your device; your follower/following lists are not uploaded to our servers.

Local data is stored using the operating system's storage and security mechanisms. Optional features under your control include biometric app lock and passphrase-protected encrypted backups (passphrase material may be kept in the device secure store).

6. Your rights

You have the right to access and delete your data. You can clear app data via Settings > Delete data, or remove local data by uninstalling the app.

7. Third-party services

Analysis of your Instagram export stays on your device. We do not upload your follower/following lists to our servers.

The free version shows ads via Google Mobile Ads (AdMob). That SDK may collect device and advertising identifiers under Google's policies; it is not used to send your Instagram lists to us. Pro removes ads.

Subscriptions use Google Play Billing. Google processes purchase and subscription status; we do not receive your payment card details. Promotional, trial, or discount codes, if offered, are created and validated by Google Play; we do not collect or store the code on our servers.

The app uses Firebase Crashlytics and Firebase Analytics (Google) to collect anonymous crash/diagnostic reports and usage analytics (for example device model, OS version, app version, stack traces, and screen or feature usage) so we can fix stability issues and improve the product. No Google or in-app account sign-in is required. These services are not used to upload your Instagram follower/following lists.

8. Contact

For questions: support@gezegenselcore.com

9. Changes

When this policy is updated, the last-updated date will change. Significant changes may be communicated via the app or the web.
