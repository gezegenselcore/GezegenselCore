2. What ReFollow is

ReFollow is a tool that analyzes Instagram "Download Your Information" export files (JSON/ZIP) that you select on your device. Analysis runs on-device. The App never accesses the Instagram app, does not log into Instagram, does not ask for your Instagram password, and is not affiliated with Instagram or Meta Platforms, Inc. Login forms shown by password apps are not the real instagram.com address.

We use the official Instagram export (ZIP) so we never need "inside" access to your account. Spending a few minutes to download and upload that file is a safer approach than handing your password to third-party apps.
