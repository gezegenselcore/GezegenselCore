Fall Asleep Faster with Relaxing Sounds 🌙 | Rain, Nature & More | Anima

A quick look at how you can layer rain, nature sounds, colored noise, and brainwave tones to build your ideal sleep and focus atmosphere.

Design Your Own Sound Mix

Anima brings together 37 ambience sounds (rain, thunderstorm, ocean waves, river, forest, fireplace, fan, hair dryer, cat purring, womb heartbeat, train, café, piano, Tibetan bowl, and more), 8 real-time calibrated noise colors (white, pink, brown, deep brown, green, blue, violet, and grey noise), and 25 sleep, relaxation, focus, and lullaby tracks under one roof. Layer up to 5 sounds with independent volume sliders, or start one of 10 ready-made mixes like Rainy Night, Beach Sleep, Deep Work, and Baby Sleep in a single tap.

Brainwave Modes & Uninterrupted Night Playback

Support your mental transitions with 7 brainwave modes — Sleep (Theta 6→4 Hz), Deep (Theta 4 Hz), Relax (Alpha 8 Hz), Anxiety Calm, Focus (Beta 16 Hz), Creativity (Theta 6 Hz), and Energize (Beta 14 Hz) — paired with an optional warm or deep drone sound bed. No headphones connected? Switch to headphone-free Isochronic mode in one tap. The sleep timer gently fades audio out over the final 60 seconds, while on-device offline playback continues smoothly with the screen off and lock-screen controls.
